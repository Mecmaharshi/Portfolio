## What I built

More than 50 FastAPI REST APIs running on Azure Functions. Serverless compute scales with demand, so traffic peaks don't require extra servers. Across my time at Celebal I also built Azure DevOps CI/CD pipelines and PyTest suites, migrated on-premises workloads to Azure, and mentored 8 junior developers.
