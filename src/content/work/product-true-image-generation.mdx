## The problem

Image models redraw everything they generate. That's fine for a background and a dealbreaker for the product. A brand cannot run an ad in which its own product's shape, logo or label has drifted, even slightly.

## The approach

Don't let the model draw the product at all. Claude Vision (on AWS Bedrock) looks at a generated scene and finds surfaces where the product can sit, then Adobe Firefly composites the **real product photo** into that spot. The scene is generated; the product is never regenerated.
