## Context

Reviewing customer calls by hand doesn't scale. This platform transcribes and analyzes every interaction, so people only have to look at what matters.

<SystemDiagram client:visible id="call-analytics" />

## What made it interesting

It had to run in two places: in the cloud and **on-premises in Docker containers**. Building one Django platform that deploys to both meant each client could choose where it runs, without a separate codebase for each.

## Results

- **50,000+ interactions a month**
- **90% accuracy**
- **70% less manual review**
